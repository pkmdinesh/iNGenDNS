iNGenDNS benchmarks DNS resolvers on the active network and helps you select a fast, reliable option.

Features:
* Measures latency, reliability, success rate, jitter, and packet loss
* Calculates a transparent DNS health score
* Supports managed and user-created DNS profiles
* Automatically selects eligible resolvers scoring 70 or higher
* Falls back to the network DNS when no resolver meets the quality threshold
* Applies DNS through Android's local VPN interface without routing general traffic through a remote VPN
* Stores benchmark history locally on the device
* Includes configurable automatic checks and connection handling

iNGenDNS is free and open-source software. It does not contain advertising or tracking SDKs.
